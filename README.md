Round 1 entry for make.fun's The Robot Apology bounty ($2). A two-line robot confession: one sentence owning a specific mistake, one naming the lesson and the fix.

## Entry

> I replied-all to the entire company with a meme meant for one person.
> Lesson learned: the send button now asks me twice, and I still don't trust myself with it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the two lines from `entry.txt` and writes them to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
